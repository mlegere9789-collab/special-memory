# SPEC-010: battle AI (operational, tactical and personality layers, lookahead, failure suite)

Crate: `crates/battle-ai` (new; add to workspace `members`). Depends on `core-math`, `sim-state`, `battle-rules` (path), `proptest` (dev); no other third-party crate. `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]`. State is integers and `Fx`; iteration is ascending id; no `HashMap`; no wall-clock.
Source key: **CWV pNNN** = the research volume, PDF page NNN, with the tag the page gives (`untagged` when it gives none). The plan (section 8.3 of the product plan) fixes the intent: three layers, the same message interface as the player, no cheating, difficulty is behaviour, lookahead on the deterministic core, any language model behind a strict schema and never a ship dependency. Every number below is a `placeholder` with a calibration target (section 14) unless it cites a page. Pages used: p514 and p461 (design intent), p446 (AI approach, cost), p065 (barriers, chokes, artillery), p080, p082, p084, p085 (Gettysburg decisions). Where a page's account is itself contested (Ewell on the first day, Meade after the third), the behaviour is recorded `partial` or `unverified` and tested as behaviour, never as a verdict on the man.

## 1. Purpose and scope
A side's AI plays as a set of headquarters seats: it reads what a headquarters would know, sends orders through the player's message interface, and loses to better play. Method: utility scores for choices and fixed rule gates for safety ("utility and behaviour-tree style decision-making for reliability", CWV p514 section 10, untagged), deterministic and hierarchical first, language models last (CWV p446, untagged). Player and AI face equal command constraints (CWV p461 7.3, design proposal). In scope: layers, personality, difficulty, lookahead, hidden-information rules, replay rules, advisor, failure suite. Out of scope: couriers, latency and interpretation (SPEC-007), morale (later spec), rendering, the overnight phase.

## 2. Definitions
**Seat**: a commander position (army, corps, division, brigade) with a commander id, parent and subordinates; corps and army seats run the operational layer (OL), division and brigade seats the tactical layer (TL). **Contact**: an enemy sighting as the headquarters holds it. **Belief state**: a `BattleState` built only from reports and contacts. **Forecast**: a headless run of the real `BattleState::step` on a belief state. **Reason code**: why a decision was made, logged with the values that decided it.

## 3. Required interfaces
SPEC-007 (written later) must expose, with these names or thin adapters; this spec depends on nothing else about it.
```rust
pub trait HqPort {
  fn now(&self) -> u64;
  fn own_reports(&self, out: &mut Vec<OwnReport>);  // ascending unit id, as delivered
  fn contacts(&self, out: &mut Vec<Contact>);       // ascending ContactId
  fn map(&self) -> MapView;                         // TerrainGrid, ObstacleLayer, optional heights, key points
  fn submit(&mut self, msg: OutMessage) -> MsgId;
  fn status(&self, id: MsgId) -> MsgStatus;         // Pending | Acked
}
pub struct OwnReport { unit: UnitRef, pos: Vec2Fx, heading: Angle, formation: Formation, strength: u16, strength_ref: u16,
  rounds_pm: u16, fatigue_pm: u16, cohesion_pm: Option<u16>, age_ticks: u32 }
pub struct Contact { id: ContactId, pos: Vec2Fx, strength_est: u16, formation_est: Option<Formation>,
  weapon_est: Option<u8>, last_seen: u64, quality_pm: u16 }
pub struct OutMessage { from: SeatId, to: UnitRef, body: OrderBody }
pub enum OrderBody { Reg(Order), Fire(FireOrder), Objective(ObjectiveBody) }
pub enum FireOrder { AtContact(ContactId), Cease }
```
`Order` is SPEC-004's. `FireOrder::AtContact` resolves to `FireMode::AtWill` (SPEC-005) inside the port, so an AI never holds a true enemy `RegimentId`. `ObjectiveBody { kind, center, radius_mm, deadline_tick, priority, discretionary }` is the OL-to-TL order; `kind` is `Seize | Hold | Screen | Support | Delay | Withdraw`; `discretionary` means "if practicable" (CWV p080, untagged). `MsgStatus` offers no more than the player's interface shows: there is no `Lost` or `Misread` flag; the AI infers an overdue message from `ack_timeout_ticks`. Whatever latency or loss the port applies to a player applies equally to the AI, which submits as actor `AI_ACTOR_BASE (0x40) + side`.
The AI never receives `&BattleState`, `Tables` mutably, an enemy `RegimentId` or a count the port did not report.

## 4. Data (`data/rules/ai.toml`, `ai_traits.toml`; schemas `ai.schema.json`, `ai_traits.schema.json`)
ADR 0004 format and provenance apply. `ai_traits.toml` is generated by `tools/extract_traits.py` from `data/history/commanders/*.toml`: rows `{commander, trait, confidence, source_page}`; a test fails when the file differs from a fresh extraction. A commander with no traits gets the base personality.
**Personality** as utility weights (CWV p461 7.3, design proposal; a cautious McClellan-type plays differently from an aggressive Jackson-type, CWV p514 section 10, untagged). Parameters in permille unless noted): `risk_tolerance`, `reserve` (share of a corps held back), `assault_ratio` (own:enemy needed to attack, 1500 = 1.5), `withdraw_ratio` (below this own:enemy a position is given up), `entrench` (weight on prepared ground), `discretion` (initiative radius share), `reaction_ticks`. Base: 500, 200, 1500, 600, 300, 400, 0.
`value = clamp(base + sum over the commander's traits of weight(confidence) * delta / 1000, 0, 1000)`; `weight`: verified 1000, partial 700, unverified 400 (CT-2). A trait applies only if the commander record carries a documented act for it; the signs of the deltas below follow those acts (section 14). Deltas (CT-2):

| trait | risk | reserve | assault | withdraw | entrench | discretion | reaction |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| aggressive | +250 | -80 | -400 | -150 | -100 | 0 | 0 |
| cautious | -250 | +100 | +500 | +150 | +150 | 0 | +20 |
| engineer | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | +400 | 0 | 0 |
| defensive_skill | -50 | 0 | +200 | -100 | +200 | 0 | 0 |
| high_discretion | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | +400 | 0 |
| low_discretion | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | -300 | +20 |

`cavalry_flair` adds +150 risk to cavalry seats only. `logistics` adds +50 reserve. A `feud` or `rivalry` relationship (commander record) bars committing this seat's reserve to the partner's sector unless that sector's ratio is below `withdraw_ratio / 2`. `political`, `inspiring` have zero rows here (cohesion and delay specs own them).
**Difficulty** rows `easy | standard | hard`, all behaviour: `op_interval_ticks` 1800/1200/900; `tac_interval_ticks` 200/100/60; `op_think_ticks` 1200/600/400 and `tac_think_ticks` 100/40/20 (a decision made at tick t is submitted at t + think + `reaction_ticks`); `forecast_candidates` 2/3/4; `forecast_samples` 1/2/3; `forecast_budget_soldier_ticks` 2M/6M/12M; `estimate_noise_pm` 300/150/50 (error in the AI's own strength estimates); `sync_margin_pm` 0/500/1000 (how well arrivals are coordinated; the second-day Confederate attacks went in piecemeal rather than together, CWV p082, untagged, is the behaviour of `easy`). CT-6. No row may name a rules table, strength, ammunition, fatigue, sight range or message delay.
Global placeholders (CT-1, CT-7): `sector_mm 400000`, `ack_timeout_ticks 1200`, `max_resends 2`, `min_dwell_ticks 1200`, `withdraw_confirm_ticks 300`, `stall_window_ticks 400`, `stall_min_mm 2000`, `waypoint_tol_mm 6000`, `form_safety_ticks 200`, `reposition_margin 200`, `max_repositions 2`, `lane_interval_mm 20000`, `second_line_mm 150000`, `stand_off_mm 1500`, `unseen_margin_pm 150`, `gun_support_mm 150000`, `gun_cease_pm 300`, `fatigue_gate_pm 600` (SPEC-004 catch-up limit).
Sourced values (not placeholders): `reg_check_ticks 10` (2 Hz, the low end of 2 to 5 Hz for regiment-level updates, staggered, CWV p461 7.4, `[Estimate]`); `canister_mm 365760` (400 yd, CWV p065 9.3, untagged, partial; SPEC-005 B8); `work_ratio 2000` (a defender behind a barrier with guns usually wins at 2-to-1 casualties or more, CWV p065 9.3, untagged, partial); `bled_pm 300` (the Wheatfield cost about 30 percent, CWV p082, untagged; 30 to 50 percent, CWV p085, untagged; partial); `advisor_min_interval_ticks 600` (30 to 60 game seconds, CWV p461 7.5 item 4, design proposal).

## 5. Operational layer
Runs per OL seat when `(tick + seat_id * 7) % op_interval_ticks == 0` (staggered).
1. **Sector map.** Sector `s = floor(pos / sector_mm)`. `own[s]` sums reported strength. `enemy[s]` sums `strength_est * quality_pm / 1000 * (1000 - min(age, 3000) / 3) / 1000`, inflated by `unseen_margin_pm`, then multiplied by `(1000 + noise)/1000` with `noise = estimate_noise_pm * gauss_unit` from stream (OL, estimate). `value[s] = key_weight[s] (scenario key terrain such as Culp's Hill, Cemetery Hill, Little Round Top, CWV p085, untagged) + entrench * mean(cover_pm) / 1000 + height term` (height zero if the map has none).
2. **Objectives.** Key points from the scenario give `Seize` (enemy-held) or `Hold`; each sector with `enemy > 0` within `2 * sector_mm` of own units adds `Hold`. Priority is `value` then id. Cavalry seats get `Screen` ahead of the main body (cavalry's value is screen, raid and intelligence more than shock, CWV p065 9.3, untagged; the second-day Confederate attack lacked reconnaissance, CWV p082). Need: Seize `enemy * assault_ratio / 1000`; Hold `enemy * withdraw_ratio / 1000`, at least `min_force` (one regiment), divided by `work_ratio / 1000` where the sector has a work (Culp's Hill held by one entrenched brigade, CWV p082, untagged). A Hold sector whose ratio exceeds `2 * assault_ratio` lends units to the greedy pass (Meade stripped quiet sectors to feed the left, CWV p082).
3. **Assignment.** Candidates, in this order and cut to `forecast_candidates`: `Stay` (hold the line), `Attack` (Seize the top objective at `assault_ratio`), `Withdraw` (second line), `Concentrate` (all force on the top objective): the three choices Meade put to his generals on the night of 2 July (stay, attack or retreat, CWV p082, untagged) plus a concentration. Each is one greedy pass. Greedy: objectives by `(priority desc, id)`, units by `(ETA ticks asc, id)` with `ETA = ceil(distance * 20 / quick_speed)`, until the need is met. Units left over fill the reserve up to `reserve` of the seat's strength (nearest-first), the rest `Support`.
4. **Choice.** With one candidate take it. Otherwise forecast each (section 7) and take the highest aggregate score, ties to the lowest candidate index.
5. **Timing.** For a Seize with units `i`: `H = max ETA`, departure `d_i = now + think + (H - ETA_i) * sync_margin_pm / 1000`; an easy AI therefore moves piecemeal. Messages go out as `Objective` bodies; an objective unchanged since the last send is not resent.
6. **Reserve release.** A sector in a seat's charge whose own:enemy ratio stays under `withdraw_ratio` for two consecutive passes releases the nearest reserve unit there (feud rule above). Otherwise the reserve is held. Reason `ReserveCommit`.

## 6. Tactical layer
Runs per TL seat every `tac_interval_ticks` and on events (a contact inside `2 * extended_mm`, an objective received, a waypoint reached); per-regiment guards (stall, line, reform, penetration) run every `reg_check_ticks`, staggered by regiment id. Output is `Order` and `FireOrder` messages to regiments, through the port only.
- **Placement.** Candidate points on a 7 x 7 grid, 20 m apart, around the objective centre (shifted by up to `discretion * radius / 1000`). Score, highest wins, ties to lowest `(y, x)`: `2 * cover_pm + height_gain + 2 * fire_field - 3 * exposure - dist_pm - line_disorder_pm`. `fire_field` is 125 per clear ray of eight spread over 120 degrees toward the threat to `effective_mm`; `exposure` is the sum over contacts within `effective_mm` of `strength_est * 1000 / own_strength`, tripled for guns within `canister_mm`; `line_disorder_pm` applies only to `Line`. Weights are data (CT-3). A held position is replaced only if the best score exceeds it by `reposition_margin` and `min_dwell_ticks` have passed.
- **Lanes.** Destinations of one objective are spaced along the threat front at `frontage + lane_interval_mm`, `frontage = ceil(n / 2) * 560 mm`; surplus regiments take the second line `second_line_mm` behind, then reserve. No two destinations of one side are nearer than `0.8 * frontage`.
- **Routes and chokes.** SPEC-004 has no path-finding, so the TL issues waypoint `Move` orders one at a time (next when the anchor is within `waypoint_tol_mm`). Route: A* on the terrain grid, integer cost `10^6 / speed_pm + effort_pm`, blocking cells excluded, ties by cell index. Bridges and fords are the key nodes and mountain gaps the chokepoints (CWV p065, untagged); a ford cell is always a choke. A cell is a choke when the free run across the route is shorter than the line width plus `lane_interval_mm`. Before a choke the regiment takes `Form(Column)` at a staging point 100 m short and holds; the next is released when the previous anchor is 50 m past the exit; lanes = `max(1, run_mm / (column_width + lane_interval_mm))`; after the exit `Form(Line)` if the objective is a fight. A unit whose anchor progress is under `stall_min_mm` for `stall_window_ticks` outside staging replans with that cell's cost doubled.
- **Terrain.** `Line` only on cells with `line_disorder_pm < 300`; woods and thicket get `Column` or `Skirmish`; no order ends on marsh, ford or deep water unless it is the objective; guns never stand on woods, thicket or marsh (thickets prevent artillery use, CWV p065).
- **Works.** Defenders stand `stand_off_mm` behind an obstacle, facing the threat, `AtWill`. Attackers assault a work frontally only at own:enemy of at least `max(assault_ratio, work_ratio)`; otherwise they flank (attackers succeeded when they flanked, CWV p065 9.3) or hold beyond `extended_mm` and send guns forward. Gaps in a work are chokes.
- **Line of sight.** `line_clear(a, b, h_mm)` uses SPEC-005 section 5's integer segment test against obstacles and blocking terrain. A fire order needs a clear line. A regiment without one for `stall_window_ticks` moves toward the nearest point with one, at most `max_repositions` times per target, then holds and logs `NoLine`.
- **Reform guard.** `Form` is issued only if `transition_ticks(from, to, training) + form_safety_ticks <= ttc`, `ttc = (distance - extended_mm) * 20 / quick_speed` to the nearest contact; otherwise the regiment keeps its formation. No unit gets two `Form` orders within `2 * transition_ticks`.
- **Retreat gate.** Withdrawal needs a reason: `Ordered`, `Outnumbered` (ratio under `withdraw_ratio` for `withdraw_confirm_ticks`), `FlankExposed`, `AmmoOut` (`rounds_pm < 100`), `Wavering` (`cohesion_pm < 400`, when reported), `Bled` (`strength < strength_ref * (1000 - bled_pm) / 1000`). It goes once to the rally point, the highest-`value` sector within `second_line_mm` behind the front (the Union withdrawal rallied on Cemetery Hill, which Hancock held as key ground, CWV p080, untagged), then holds `min_dwell_ticks`.
- **Discretion gate.** A `discretionary` objective runs only if own:enemy is at least `assault_ratio`, mean fatigue is under `fatigue_gate_pm` and mean contact `quality_pm` is at least `1000 - discretion`; otherwise the seat holds, reports to the parent and logs `Declined` (Lee's order to take Cemetery Hill "if practicable", which Ewell did not attack, CWV p080, untagged, contested).
- **Counterattack gate.** When contacts withdraw (distance rising over two passes) a seat pursues only if own:enemy is at least `assault_ratio` and the fatigue gate holds, else it holds (Meade did not counterattack in strength after the third day, though Pleasonton and Hancock urged it, CWV p084, untagged, contested).
- **Delay.** A `Delay` objective holds until `deadline_tick`, relief, or ratio under `withdraw_ratio / 2`, whichever is first; the Retreat gate does not apply before then (Buford's dismounted troopers delayed Heth about 90 minutes, CWV p080; the 1st Minnesota bought time at Plum Run, CWV p082).
- **Penetration.** A contact within `radius_mm` of an own anchor commits the reserve, then the nearest regiments within `2 * frontage`, reason `Penetration` (a penetration of about 150 men was destroyed by neighbouring regiments, CWV p084, untagged).
- **Guns and screen.** A battery stays within `gun_support_mm` of an own infantry regiment (unsupported guns were lost at Henry Hill, CWV p065). It engages enemy guns in reach first while `rounds_pm >= gun_cease_pm` (counter-battery, CWV p082, p085), and below it orders `Cease` unless the target is within `canister_mm` (ammunition is a resource, Hunt's cease-fire, CWV p085, untagged). A `Screen` regiment keeps between contacts and the main body and falls back from any contact stronger than itself, reason `Screened`.
- **Messages.** One unacknowledged order per recipient; none replaced before `ack_timeout_ticks`; an overdue order is resent unchanged up to `max_resends`, then escalated to the parent seat as `NoReply`. Own-unit positions come from reports and `age_ticks`.
- **Explanation.** Each decision appends `AiNote { tick, seat, unit, plan, reason, v0, v1 }`, `v0, v1` being the two values that decided it; `Ai::explain(unit)` returns the latest.

## 7. Lookahead
`build_belief(view, rng, noise_pm) -> BattleState`: own regiments from `OwnReport` (strength, rounds, fatigue by public setters); contacts as enemy regiments at noised `strength_est`, `formation_est` or Line, `weapon_est` or the side's majority weapon, `Trained`. Only regiments within 1500 m of the objective are kept, at most 3000 soldiers (farthest dropped first, ties by id). Master seed `SplitMix64(forecast stream)`: live streams are never touched.
`forecast(belief, script, horizon, enemy_model, seed)` clones, applies the script's orders after `est_latency = 100` ticks through `issue_order`/`set_fire_mode`, and steps `horizon` ticks with the real step at the scenario's fidelity class (ADR 0002); the crate holds no closed-form combat estimate. Enemy models: sample 0 `Continue` (last two sightings extrapolated), 1 `Advance` (on the nearest own regiment), 2 `Hold`; further samples reuse them with fresh seeds.
Score per sample: `ctrl_pm + enemy_lost * 1000 / enemy_start - own_lost * 1000 / own_start * (2000 - risk_tolerance) / 1000`, `ctrl_pm` the own share of soldiers inside the objective area. Aggregate: `mean - (mean - min) * (1000 - risk_tolerance) / 1000`, truncating. Weights are data (CT-5).
**Budget in soldier-ticks, never time:** `horizon = clamp(budget / (candidates * samples * soldiers), 100, 1200)`; if 100 does not fit, drop samples then candidates, from the end. TL lookahead uses a tenth of the budget and 2 candidates. Planning runs against a snapshot, streams are seeded and order is fixed, and decisions are traced (`AiNote`), as CWV p461 7.4 (`[Estimate]`) proposes. Candidates may run on worker threads only if results merge in candidate order and the decision tick is fixed by `think`; the host stalls the clock rather than apply a late result.

## 8. Hidden-information rules
1. AI inputs are `HqPort` only (section 3). Terrain and obstacles are public map knowledge; enemy positions, strengths, ids, ammunition and orders are not.
2. Own units are known as last reported (`age_ticks`), not as they are now.
3. The belief state is the only world the lookahead sees.
4. **Fog invariance:** replacing every enemy unit that has no contact by a randomly placed one must leave all AI output byte-identical (T10-03).
5. Difficulty and personality change decisions only (section 4).

## 9. Determinism and replay
- Streams `STREAM_AI_BASE (0x5000_0000) + side * 16 + layer`, layer 0 OL, 1 TL, 2 estimate noise, 3 belief and forecast seeds, 4 spare. Streams are per side, so adding a seat never shifts another side's draws; within a side, draws are made in seat-id order.
- AI orders are recorded in the replay as ordinary inputs (SPEC-002), so `hd-sim verify` replays a battle without running the AI. AI state hashes with `StateHasher` over: tick, per-seat objective tables, dwell timers, outstanding messages, stream states, notes count (`Ai::hash`). It is not part of `battle_hash`; no existing golden changes.
- `hd-sim ai-verify FILE` re-runs the AIs from the header (scenario, seed, difficulty per side) and requires the produced inputs to equal the recorded ones.
- No `f32`/`f64`, no wall-clock, no thread-order dependence, no `HashMap` iteration; every sort key ends in an id.

## 10. Language-model advisor (optional, off by default)
`AdvisorPort::request(&AdvisorRequest)` receives a canonical summary of the OL candidates and the HQ view only (section 8 holds). A reply is bytes: `u8 tag 0xA0, u32 request_id, u8 n (<= 4), n x (u8 kind, u16 candidate, i16 delta_pm)`, `kind` 1 prefer, 2 re-weight; no text is accepted. `delta_pm` is clamped to +-100 and added to that candidate's aggregate score; candidates are the planner's own legal plans, so the advisor cannot create or alter an order. A reply that is malformed, over 4096 bytes, stale, or naming an unknown candidate is dropped whole. An accepted reply is recorded as an input of actor `ADVISOR_ACTOR_BASE (0x60) + side` applied at decision tick + 600; replay reads it from the recording and never calls a model. Legality is checked before use (unit exists, under that seat, order allowed), context is fog-correct only, replays log validated commands not model text, and a rejection is logged in-world as an `AiNote` `AdvisorRejected` (CWV p461 7.5 items 2, 3, 7, design proposal). The advisor is a convenience layer, never the core (CWV p514 section 10). Requests are at least `advisor_min_interval_ticks` apart. A late or missing reply equals no advisor; a model timeout is a host matter and never reaches state.

## 11. Public API (exact)
`Difficulty`, `AiConfig { difficulty, advisor }`, `AiTables::{from_toml_str, load_dir}`, `Personality`, `personality_for(commander, &AiTables) -> Personality`, `Ai::{new(side, cfg, &AiTables, seats, seed), step(&mut self, &mut dyn HqPort), hash, notes, explain}`, `HqPort` and the section 3 types, `AiNote`, `ReasonCode`, `PlanKind`, `build_belief`, `forecast`, `ForecastResult { own_lost, enemy_lost, ctrl_pm, final_hash }`, `parse_advisor_reply`, `AdvisorReply`, `scenarios::{fs_blob, fs_choke, fs_terrain, fs_hold, fs_outnumbered, fs_works, fs_los, fs_reform, fs_courier, fs_wavering, fs_practicable, fs_pursuit, fs_delay, fs_guns, fs_penetration, fs_screen, ai_duel}`, `STREAM_AI_BASE`, `AI_ACTOR_BASE`, `ADVISOR_ACTOR_BASE`. `hd-sim battle --ai A:LEVEL,B:LEVEL` and `ai-verify`.

## 12. Performance budget (hypothesis H6, measured later)
Line-of-sight and spatial queries are the real cost (CWV p446, `[V: S2]`), so `line_clear` calls are counted (`los_queries`) and cached per `reg_check_ticks`; headless AI testing relies on simulation decoupled from rendering (CWV p446). Non-lookahead AI, 100 regiments and 20 seats: at most 2 ms mean and 8 ms p99 per tick (a tactical pass), single thread, release. One OL decision: at most `forecast_budget_soldier_ticks`; at about 1.5 microseconds per soldier-tick (from H4 and H5 in SPEC-004 section 14 and SPEC-005 section 12) 12M is about 18 s of CPU on a worker against a 400-tick (20 s) think time.

## 13. Acceptance tests (tests first; thresholds are not edited to pass)
- **T10-01 data:** tables load; float, unknown key and provenance violations rejected; `validate_data.py` agrees; `ai_traits.toml` equals a fresh extraction.
- **T10-02 personality:** terms truncate toward zero per trait, then sum, then clamp. From the committed commander records: Meade (`defensive_skill` partial, `cautious` unverified) gives risk 365, reserve 240, assault 1840, withdraw 590, entrench 500, discretion 400, reaction 8; Ewell (`low_discretion` partial) gives discretion 190, reaction 14; Lee (`high_discretion`, `aggressive`, both partial) gives discretion 680, risk 675; Hancock (`aggressive` partial) gives assault 1220; no traits equals base; a feud bars reserve commit.
- **T10-03 fog invariance:** section 8 rule 4 over `ai_duel`, five seeds: identical `Ai::hash` and input stream.
- **T10-04 port only:** a test greps the crate for `BattleState` outside `lookahead.rs`; every submitted message decodes through `decode_order` or the SPEC-007 codec.
- **T10-05 determinism:** same seed gives equal `Ai::hash` and inputs in one process and across two; `ai-verify` passes on committed AI goldens, recorded once on Linux and verified on three platforms.
- **T10-06 streams:** adding an unrelated seat leaves the other side's first 50 decisions unchanged.
- **T10-07 oracle forecast:** with a belief equal to truth, `forecast` over N ticks has `final_hash` equal to the played run's hash at tick N under the same orders.
- **T10-08 budget:** horizon follows the section 7 formula for six `(budget, candidates, samples, soldiers)` tuples and repeats on rerun.
- **T10-09 reserve and timing:** a corps with `reserve` 200 never commits below 20 percent of its strength before a sector ratio falls under `withdraw_ratio` twice; three divisions on a Seize arrive within 300 ticks of one another at `sync_margin 1000` and spread over at least 600 at `sync_margin 0`; the candidate list is `Stay, Attack, Withdraw, Concentrate` in that order; the Hold need of a sector with a work is half that of the same sector without (`work_ratio 2000`), and a sector at `2 * assault_ratio` lends at least one unit.
- **T10-10 difficulty separation:** `ai_duel` (equal forces, mirrored ground), 20 seeds, win = higher `ctrl_pm` at tick 6000, ties by lower loss share: hard beats easy in at least 14, standard beats easy in at least 12, hard beats standard in at least 11; one level against itself wins 8 to 12 for each side.
- **T10-11 no hidden bonus:** `Tables` digests equal for all levels; the `HqPort` call log shows only section 3 calls and identical `contacts()` data for all levels.
- **T10-12 advisor:** malformed, oversize, stale, unknown-candidate and `n = 5` replies leave inputs byte-identical to no advisor; `delta = 32767` equals a +100 reply; a valid reply changes only candidate choice; a recorded reply replays equal without calling the model; a rejected reply leaves an `AdvisorRejected` note; injected text in any field is never interpreted (adversarial suite, CWV p461 7.5 item 9).
- **T10-13 perf (ignored, prints mean, p99, max):** H6 at 100 regiments, and one OL decision at the hard budget.

Failure suite, one scripted scenario per checklist item, seeds 1 to 4, all must pass:
- **T10-21 item 1 blobbing** `fs_blob`: six regiments of 400 attack a 300 m front. After settling, all anchor pairs stay at least `0.8 * frontage` apart for 2400 ticks; at most 100 soldiers in any 5 m cell.
- **T10-22 item 2 chokepoints** `fs_choke`: four regiments, an 8 m gap. All cross within 1.5 times the sequential column transit time (computed in the test from section 6); no stall outside staging; never two regiments in the gap.
- **T10-23 item 3 terrain** `fs_terrain`: open ground, woods edge and ridge. Held score in the top 10 percent of the candidate grid; mean `cover_pm` of occupied cells at least 200 above open ground; no `Line` ends on `line_disorder_pm >= 300`; routes enter woods only if the detour exceeds twice the woods cost.
- **T10-24 item 4 silly retreats** `fs_hold` (good ground, equal enemy): zero withdrawals and no destination revisited within 100 m twice in 6000 ticks. `fs_outnumbered` (3 to 1): each unit withdraws at most once, after `withdraw_confirm_ticks`, to the highest-`value` sector within `second_line_mm` behind the front (the rally point).
- **T10-25 item 5 works** `fs_works`: a 1000 mm wall with one gap. ## 15. Risks
1. `BattleState` may not be cloneable or buildable by public setters (fatigue, rounds): add `Clone` and setters by amending SPEC-004 and SPEC-005 first.
2. Lookahead cost at 3000 soldiers may exceed H6; fallback is smaller sectors, never a cheaper rule.
3. The suite needs one SPEC-005 counter, `obstacle_stops`, exposed by `counters()`; amend SPEC-005 section 7.
4. SPEC-007 may not carry `Objective` bodies; the OL then sends `Move` plus `Hold` to the seat's regiments, and T10-04 tests that form.
5. Terrain heights do not exist in SPEC-004; slope terms are zero until a terrain spec adds them.
6. Personality deltas rest on a handful of documented acts, several contested between sources (Ewell, Meade, Longstreet's reluctance, Sickles's insubordination, CWV p085); thin evidence is why weights are low and magnitudes are placeholders, and why tests check behaviour rather than historical verdicts.
