# SPEC-009: battle-rules III, smoke, weather and sight

> Shared namespaces (input tags and actors, random streams, flags, hash blocks, tick order, CT ids, amendment labels, ticket ids) are owned by [REGISTRY.md](REGISTRY.md); conflicts and their resolutions are in [CONFLICTS.md](CONFLICTS.md).

Crate: `crates/battle-rules`; new module `environment` (weather, heat, rain, sight, observers) and an extension of the `smoke` module that SPEC-005 section 8 introduces. Same conventions, data units and provenance as SPEC-004 sections 1 and 2; the same `DataError`. Rendering is out of scope.
Source key: **CWV pNNN** = "The American Civil War: Research Volume and Game Design", PDF page NNN, with the tag the page gives. Invented numbers are `placeholder` with a calibration target CT-009-n (section 10). Coordinates: x east, y north, positions `Vec2Fx` metres; integer mm below.

## 1. Purpose and scope
Black-powder smoke is a gameplay system: a density field that wind moves and dissipates, that blocks line of sight, hides movement, confuses command and signals and forced fighting at much shorter range than the weapons allowed (CWV p511 4.3). A coarse CPU grid, coupled to gameplay by optical depth, keeps what the player sees and what the simulation sees in agreement (CWV p451 3.4); smoke obscured the fall of the Gettysburg cannonade (CWV p083). This spec fixes the smoke grid and its update, the weather timeline, heat and rain effects, line of sight, the targeting gate and small-feature rule, headquarters sighting, hashing, budget and calibration. Morale is a later spec; section 8 publishes its inputs.
**Environment off.** `BattleState.env: Option<Environment>`. With `None`, every SPEC-004 and SPEC-005 behaviour and golden hash is unchanged, bit for bit. Everything below applies only when `Some`.

## 2. Assumptions
A1 Smoke is one 2D column layer (`height_mm` of air per cell). A2 One wind vector for the whole field. A3 Relief changes sight, not ballistics: projectiles keep SPEC-005's flat datum (`z` is height above local ground). A4 The smoke cell equals the terrain cell. A5 Times are local clock. A6 Fidelity class does not change any rule here.

## 4. Smoke
`SmokeGrid` is SPEC-005's (`density: Vec<u32>`, row-major, row 0 south) with the cell size, width, height and origin of the terrain grid. Constants are read from `[smoke]`.
**Source.** Per shot, immediately in the fire phase: `add_smoke(muzzle_pos, powder_mg, misfire: bool)` adds `powder_mg * yield_pm / 1000` (misfire times `misfire_pm / 1000`, integer floor) to the muzzle cell, saturating at `cell_cap`; the saturated excess goes to `lost_cap`.
**Update** at the end of the tick when `tick % period_ticks == period_ticks - 1`, a pure function of the snapshot `s` (the order of cells cannot matter): wind cells per update `cx = |wind_x| * period_ticks * 10^6 / (TICK_HZ * cell_mm)` (ppm, mm/s in, floor), `cy` likewise. For each cell `i` ascending with `d = s[i] > 0`, `out_pm = 1000 - retain_pm(class(i))`:
`fx = d * cx * out_pm / 10^9`, `fy = d * cy * out_pm / 10^9`, `fd = d * diffuse_ppm * out_pm / 10^9` (u64, floors).
`dst[i] += d - fx - fy - 4 * fd`; `fx` moves to the east neighbour if `wind_x > 0` else west; `fy` north if `wind_y > 0` else south; `fd` to each of four neighbours. Flow beyond the edge is added to `lost_edge`. Then per cell, with `dr = decay_ppm * (1000 + rain_pm * rain_decay_gain_pm / 1000) / 1000`: `lost_decay += ceil(dst * dr / 10^6)`, `dst -= that`. Mass is exactly conserved: injected = sum(density) + `lost_edge` + `lost_decay` + `lost_cap` (u64 counters, hashed). Ceiling decay guarantees every cell reaches 0.
The update visits only the bounding rectangle of non-zero cells grown by one cell; this equals the full-grid result (T9-07).
**Concentration** `smoke_pm(cell) = min(1000, d * 1000 / ref_units)`. SPEC-005's `smoke_at_shooter` is `smoke_pm` of the shooter's cell. With `env = Some` this section replaces SPEC-005 section 8 (source, decay and `SMOKE_UNITS_PER_G`); with `env = None` SPEC-005 section 8 applies unchanged (SPEC-005 amendment A8, REGISTRY section 9).

## 5. Sight
**Relief.** `ElevationGrid { cell_mm, width, height, origin, h_mm: Vec<i32> }` from the sidecar `data/scenarios/<family>/relief.toml` (`rows`, arrays of integer decimetres, row 0 south; the SPEC-008 scenario file is closed to unknown keys; the source of the heights is open decision D-02 in CONFLICTS); `ground_mm(p)` is bilinear between the four nearest cell centres, `i128`, floor division, clamped at the edges; a scenario without relief is flat 0. Positions convert by `to_mm(Fx) = floor(raw * 1000 / 2^32)`.
**Query** `sight(a: Vec2Fx, eye_a: i32, b: Vec2Fx, eye_b: i32, step_mm) -> Sight { clearance_mm: i64, transmit_pm: u16, visible: bool }`, heights above local ground. `L = isqrt(dx^2 + dy^2)` in mm; `za = ground(a) + eye_a`, `zb = ground(b) + eye_b`. Samples `s_k = k * step_mm`, `k = 1 ..` while `s_k < L`. Sample point `p_k = a + d * s_k / L` (`i128`, truncating), ray height `z_k = za + (zb - za) * s_k / L`.
1. Relief: `clearance = min over k of (z_k - ground(p_k))`.
2. Obstacles (SPEC-005 `ObstacleLayer`): each obstacle segment crossing `a..b` (exact `i128` cross products, endpoints inclusive) at fraction `t = num/den` gives `z_t = za + (zb - za) * num / den`; `clearance = min(clearance, z_t - ground(X) - height_mm)`.
3. Smoke: each interval `[s_{k-1}, s_k]` (the last one truncated at `L`) adds `tau_micro += density(cell of its midpoint) * len_mm * ext_k / 1000`. `transmit_ppm = exp_table(tau_micro)`; `transmit_pm = transmit_ppm / 1000`.
`visible = clearance >= clear_margin_mm && transmit_pm >= min_transmit_pm`. `exp_table` has 129 entries of `round(10^6 * exp(-i/8))`, linear interpolation at `tau_micro / 125000`, 0 beyond `tau = 16`; committed as a const array generated by `qc/fixtures/ref_exp.py` (Python `decimal`, 50 digits) with a test that regenerates it.
Infantry queries use `step_mm`; observers use `far_step_mm`.
**Eye and aim heights.** Shooter muzzle height by posture (SPEC-005); target aim point `0.6 * posture height` (standing 1020 mm). A soldier behind a 1000 mm wall is therefore seen standing and not prone.
`signal_quality_pm(a, eye_a, b, eye_b) -> u16`: `transmit_pm` if `clearance >= 0` and the distance is within `clear_vis_mm * light_pm / 1000`, else 0. The command layer scales courier, flag and telegraph-free signalling by it: dense smoke "confuses command and signals" (CWV p511 4.3).

## 6. Targeting gate and the small-feature rule
Replaces the target choice in SPEC-005 section 4 (**SPEC-005 amendment A9**, which fixes the retry count, what counts as blind and the counters). A Ready shooter `j` of `n` target roster entries tries entries `(j + k) % n` for `k = 0 ..= retries` in order, using the frozen snapshot, and takes the first with `sight(...).visible` and within `extended_mm`. If none, the soldier **holds fire**: no round spent, no draw from the soldier stream (a shot's draws stay fixed in count and order, SPEC-005 section 3), `blind` set. The range estimate error of SPEC-005 section 3 is multiplied by `(1000 + range_smoke_gain_pm * (1000 - transmit_pm) / 1000) / 1000`, so thin smoke widens errors before it blocks.
**Small features.** A rise or wall smaller than the 8 m grid blocks some men and not their neighbours. Relief is bilinear (no stair steps); walls are obstacles with their own heights; a margin `clear_margin_mm` suppresses grazing flicker. The behaviour is the period's: every `sight.period_ticks`, for each soldier ascending in an `AtWill` regiment in `Steady(Line|Skirmish)`: if `blind` and `peek_mm < peek_max_mm`, `peek_mm += peek_step_mm`; if it neither fired nor was blind this period, `peek_mm -= min(peek_mm, peek_back_mm)`; then `blind`, `fired` are cleared. A `Move` order sets the `peek_mm` of the regiment's soldiers to 0 (and the regiment's own `peek_mm`, the largest of them). **SPEC-004 amendment A6 (section 9):** the steering target is `anchor + right * u + forward * (v + peek_mm)`; slot assignment is unchanged. Men thus walk forward a step at a time to a clear shot, with no path search. Regiment `view_blocked = true` when at least `blind_report_pm` permille of its soldiers hold `peek_max_mm` and are still blind; the AI and command layer read it.

**State.** Per soldier `peek_mm: u16`, `blind`, `fired` live in `LocoExt`; per regiment `view_blocked` and `peek_mm` in `Regiment`; `Environment` gains `relief: ElevationGrid` (flat until `Environment::set_relief`) and `inputs: Vec<EnvInputs>`, one per regiment, and `BattleState::{env_inputs(id), view_blocked(id)}` read them. The period step (TK-13) visits regiments ascending and soldiers ascending, and clears `blind` and `fired` of every soldier whether or not its regiment is `AtWill`; `view_blocked` counts the soldiers with `peek_mm >= peek_max_mm` and `blind` before they are cleared, against `roster_len` (false for an empty roster); `peek_mm` is clamped to `peek_max_mm`. `EnvInputs`: `smoke_pm` is `smoke_pm` at the anchor, `blind_pm` is `blind count * 1000 / roster_len` before clearing, `heat_drain_pm` the current `drain_pm` (all `u16`).

## 7. Weather effects
**Heat.** `apparent_f10 = temp + max(0, temp - floor_f10) * max(0, humidity_pm - humid_floor_pm) / div`. `drain_pm`, `rest_pm` come from the curve by linear interpolation, clamped at the ends, as in SPEC-004 `fatigue_speed_pm`. **SPEC-004 amendment A5 (section 4):** moving: `fatigue += drain * effort_pm * drain_pm / 10^6` (floor; equals the old formula at 1000); resting: `fatigue -= rest_recover * rest_pm / 1000`; SPEC-005's firing drain is multiplied by `drain_pm / 1000`.
**Rain.** Per period: powder damp `p += rain_pm * powder_rise_ppm / 1000` if `rain_pm > 0` else `p -= powder_fall_ppm`, clamped `0..=10^6`; ground wetness `g` likewise with the ground rates. `Weather.wet_pm = 1000 + p * wet_extra_pm / 10^6` (SPEC-005 misfire factor); `Weather.ground_pm = 1000 - g * (1000 - ground_min_pm) / 10^6`; `Weather.wind` = `WeatherNow` wind.
**State and refresh.** `Environment { table: EnvTable, weather: WeatherTable, now: WeatherNow, damp: u32, ground: u32, heat: HeatNow }`; `HeatNow { apparent_f10, drain_pm, rest_pm }`. `damp` and `ground` are ppm and start at 0. `BattleState::set_environment(table, weather)` stores an `Environment` whose `now`, `heat` and `BattleState.weather` are evaluated for the period the current tick lies in (`weather.at(tick - tick % period)`, no rain step), so the first tick is consistent; `env = None` is set by assigning the field. Step TK-02 runs when `tick % smoke.period_ticks == 0`: `now = weather.at(tick)`, one rain step on `damp` and `ground` from `now.rain_pm`, `heat` from `now`, then `BattleState.weather = Weather { wind: now wind in m/s, wet_pm, ground_pm }` (mm/s to `Fx` metres per second by `fx_ratio(mm_s, 1000)`). Interpolation of the heat curve truncates toward zero like `fatigue_speed_pm`. The firing drain of SPEC-005 is `fire_drain_ppm * drain_pm / 1000` (floor), then clamped at `FATIGUE_MAX`; every soldier of both teams takes the same multipliers. The hash block of `damp`, `ground` and `now` belongs to the smoke-grid ticket (section 9).

## 8. Headquarters sight and morale inputs
`Observer { id: u16, team: u8, pos: Vec2Fx, eye_mm: i32, vision_mm: u32 }`. Every regiment is an observer at its anchor (id = regiment id, eye 1700, vision 3000000); `add_observer` registers headquarters and signal stations (ids from 0x8000; a hilltop's height comes from relief). Effective range `vision_mm.min(clear_vis_mm * light_pm / 1000)`. Observer `o` runs when `tick % 20 == o.id % 20` (once per period): for each enemy regiment within range, ascending id, it tests `min(observer_samples, roster_len)` soldiers at roster indices `k * len / m` and records `Sighting { observer: u16, enemy: RegimentId, tick: u64, men_seen: u16, quality_pm: u16, centroid: Vec2Fx }` in the team's `SightLog` (one slot per enemy regiment, newest wins) if any sample is visible: `men_seen = len * visible / m`; `quality_pm = mean over visible of transmit_pm * (1000 - dist * 1000 / range) / 1000`; `centroid` the mean of visible positions. The log is the only thing headquarters may read about the enemy; staleness is `tick - sighting.tick`.
Morale inputs, refreshed each period per regiment: `EnvInputs { smoke_pm: u16 (anchor cell), blind_pm: u16 (share of soldiers blind), view_blocked: bool, heat_drain_pm: u16 }`.

## 9. Order of work, hash, API

Hash: SPEC-005's smoke block hashes `density` as it does now. When `env` is `Some`, after the casualty block and after the morale and command blocks (REGISTRY section 5) write `u32 0x45_4E_56_31`, then `WeatherNow` (`i32` fields, wind `i64`), damp `u32`, ground `u32`, `lost_edge`, `lost_decay`, `lost_cap` (`u64`), per regiment ascending `EnvInputs` (`smoke_pm u16, blind_pm u16, view_blocked u8, heat_drain_pm u16`), `peek_mm u16, blind u8, fired u8` per slot ascending, observer list, and each team's `SightLog` entries ascending. `None` writes nothing.

## 12. Performance budget (hypothesis H9)
Added to SPEC-005's H5: environment work at most 2 ms mean per tick and 6 ms p99 in release, single thread, inside the 50 ms tick. Parts: smoke update over a 40,000-cell active rectangle about 2 ms in its tick (0.4 ms amortised); the full 750 by 625 grid at most 12 ms in its tick; the gate about 45 queries per tick of about 75 samples (under 0.3 ms); observers about 4,000 rays a second of about 500 samples (about 1 ms mean). Hypotheses until T9-18 is recorded in a cycle report.

## Amendment A2 (from T-097 calibration)
The first run of the environment bands (CYCLE-environment) missed E2 (transmission between 91 m lines in a 3 m/s crosswind, 17 permille against 200 to 900) because the placeholder yield keeps the line's own muzzle cells opaque even when the wind carries smoke along the line. The row below was fitted on data only; no band, formula or test threshold changed. It stays a placeholder with its target.

| Row | Old | New | Band | Target |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `[smoke]` `yield_pm` | 550 | 220 | E2, with E1, E3 and E4 re-checked | CT-009-1 |

Why plausible: the model keeps all smoke in one 3 m column, but a volley's plume expands and rises well above 3 m within seconds, so the share of the powder mass that stays in the layer that blocks sight is below the solid-residue share of the powder (the old value). The figure is unsourced either way (section 3.1).

**Scenario parameter.** The scenario `crest` of band E7 uses a crest of 1.5 m (`crest_dm = 15`), not 3 m. A soldier's eye is 1.7 m above his ground and the target aim point 1.02 m, so a ray over a crest higher than the shooter's own rise cannot clear it: with the crest ramp 68 to 132 m and a peek of at most `peek_max_mm`, 1.5 m gives every man a shot, 1.6 m about half, 1.7 m and above none. **Measurement window of E5.** E5 reads fatigue after 900 s at quick time, not after 1 h; the limits 1100 to 1500 permille are unchanged.
