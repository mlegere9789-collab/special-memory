# SPEC-007: command friction and the information model

> Shared namespaces (input tags and actors, random streams, flags, hash blocks, tick order, CT ids, amendment labels, ticket ids) are owned by [REGISTRY.md](REGISTRY.md); conflicts and their resolutions are in [CONFLICTS.md](CONFLICTS.md).

Module `command` in `crates/battle-rules` (SPEC-004 conventions: integers only, ascending ids, no `HashMap`, integer-only TOML with provenance, ADR 0004). Source key: **CWV pNNN** = PDF page NNN (pages used here are `untagged`). Invented numbers are `placeholder` with a calibration target (CT-007-n, section 10, numbered locally as in SPEC-004 and SPEC-005).

## 1. Purpose and scope
Couriers can be killed, delayed or confused; flags and telegraph work where lines exist; the player orders corps and divisions, who pass orders down and interpret them by trait (CWV p511 4.7). Discretion is the freedom to interpret (CWV p508 3.4). Intelligence is delayed, partial and shown with confidence (CWV p508 3.5). This spec defines messages, the chain, each officer's picture, what player and AI may see, status feedback (genre checklist item 11) and replay encoding. Out of scope: strategic messages, flag interception (CWV p086), spies, balloons, tactical AI choices.

## 2. Definitions
- **Officer**: a node of `Army(0) > Corps(1) > Division(2) > Brigade(3) > Regiment(4)`; a Regiment officer owns one `RegimentId`, higher officers the union beneath them (the *formation*).
- **Message**: `Order`, `Ack` or `Report` sent by a **medium** (`Courier=1`, `Flags=2`, `Telegraph=3`); a **leg** is one courier journey.
- **Picture**: an officer's contacts (enemy) and friends with confidence in permille; the player's picture is his seat officer's.
- **Latitude**: `Strict=0` or `Discretionary=1` ("if practicable", CWV p080).
- `to_mm(Fx) = ((raw as i128 * 1000) >> 32) as i64`; `dist_mm(a, b) = to_mm((b - a).length())`. `ceil_div` is integer ceiling on `i128`. Times are ticks (`TICK_HZ = 20`). `scale32(w: u32, n: u32) = ((w as u64 * n as u64) >> 32) as u32`; `hit(w, ppm) = w % 1_000_000 < ppm`.

## 4. State
`CommandState { opt: [CommandOptions; 2], officers, links, msgs, records: Vec<OrderRecord>, next_msg: u32, pics: Vec<Picture> }`, ordered by id. `Officer { id, side, level, parent, regiment, mask, deputy, down: bool, pos: Vec2Fx, hq_fixed: Option<Vec2Fx>, seq: u32, last_seq_from: Vec<(OfficerId, u32)>, last_report: u64 }`. `CommandOptions { omniscient_view, instant_orders }` (default false). `Message { id, kind, from, to, medium, seq, body: OrderBody, words, root: MessageId, leg_from: Vec2Fx, aim: Vec2Fx, legs: u8, due: u64, fate: InFlight|Lost|Captured, garbled: Option<GarbleKind>, rng: [u64; 4], payload }` with `OrderBody { order: Order, latitude }`. Officer positions refresh every `pos_interval_ticks`: `hq_fixed`, else the integer mean (`i128`, truncating) of anchors of regiments with a non-empty roster in his formation.
`BattleState` gains `cmd: Option<CommandState>`. `None` leaves every SPEC-004 and SPEC-005 behaviour and hash unchanged. When `Some`, direct regiment inputs (wire tags 1 to 5) from human or AI seats are refused (`InputError::DirectOrder`) unless the seat's `instant_orders` is set.

## 5. Messages and latency
`words(order)`: Hold 4, Face 5, Form 5, Move 12. `dispatch(from, to, medium: Option<Medium>, order, latitude) -> Result<MessageId, DispatchError>` needs `from` not down and on the actor's side, `to` any officer of that side. It creates an `OrderRecord`, assigns `seq = ++officer.seq` and sends. With `instant_orders` delivery is next tick, with no draws and no loss.
**Estimate** `estimate(from, to, medium, words) -> (eta_lo, eta_hi)` (pure; shown to the player at send), `t0` the send tick:
- Courier: `eta_lo = t0 + write + base + leg` where `leg = ceil_div(D * path_factor_pm * 20, speed_mm_s * m)`, `D = dist_mm(from.pos, to.pos)` and `m = max(min_terrain_pm, floor(sum of terrain speed_pm at the eight points from + (to - from) * (2i+1)/16, i = 0..7, divided by 8))`.
- Flags: needs a `[[link]]` with `dist_mm <= flag_range_mm`; `eta_lo = t0 + write + base + per_word * words`.
- Telegraph: a link not cut; `eta_lo = t0 + write + base + per_word * words`, independent of distance.
- `eta_hi = eta_lo + jitter_ticks` (+ `smoke_max_waits * smoke_wait_ticks` for flags). `medium = None` picks the smallest `eta_lo` among usable media (ties to the lower medium number); courier is always usable.
**Per leg draws**, fixed order from the message stream (`StreamId(0x5100_0000 + id)`, section 8): `w1` jitter (`scale32(w1, jitter_ticks + 1)` added to `due`), `w2` loss, `w3` capture split. Loss probability `p = min(loss_cap_ppm, loss_base + loss_ppm_per_km * (D * path_factor_pm / 1000) / 10^6 + exposure_ppm_per_enemy * min(exposure_cap, n))` for couriers (others: `loss_base`), `n` = enemy agents in `grid.query_segment(from, aim, exposure_halfwidth_mm)`. `fate = Lost` if `hit(w2, p)`, and `Captured` instead when also `n > 0` and `w3 % 1000 < capture_share_pm`. A lost or captured message never arrives and nothing tells the sender. A captured order adds, after one leg's time, a contact at its destination to the nearest enemy officer's picture (lowest id on ties), strength = sender formation strength, quality `capture_quality_pm`.
**Flags** wait while smoke density at either station exceeds `smoke_limit` (`SmokeGrid::density_at(pos) -> u32`, SPEC-005 amendment A6, REGISTRY section 9): re-test every `smoke_wait_ticks`, at most `smoke_max_waits` times, then fall back to courier. Smoke confuses signals (CWV p511 4.3). A cut telegraph falls back to courier at send.
**Arrival** at `due`: a courier whose recipient now stands further than `find_radius_mm` from `aim` and `legs < max_legs` starts another leg from `aim` to the recipient's present position (three more draws). A recipient that is `down` gets the message redirected to his deputy after `redirect_ticks`, or it is lost without one (Reynolds fell and Doubleday took over, CWV p080).
**Reading** (Order only), draws in fixed order: `r1` garble roll, `r2` kind roll, two `gauss_unit` for the displacement, then used as follows. Garbled iff `hit(r1, garble_ppm)`. Move: `r2 % 1000 < garble_move_displace_pm` displaces the target by `garble_sigma_mm * gauss` per axis, `< garble_move_gait_pm` shifts the gait by +1 or -1 (sign `r2 >> 31`, clamped to walk..double_quick), else becomes `Hold` (`Stalled`). Form: `< garble_form_swap_pm` selects another formation (`+1` or `+2` modulo three by sign), else `Stalled`. Face: `< garble_face_turn_pm` turns by `garble_angle_deg` degrees times the first gauss, else `Stalled`. Hold is never garbled. Orders bear their hour: a `seq` not above `last_seq_from[sender]` is `Stale` and discarded.

## 6. The chain and reinterpretation
After reading, the recipient finishes at `ready = now + staff_ticks(level) * process_pm / 1000 + scale32(w, staff_jitter_ticks + 1)` (`w` the next stream word; `process_pm` the product of the mask's trait values, divided by 1000 per factor). At `ready`, `interpret` runs, then:
1. An `Ack` goes to the sender carrying `received` (the body after garbling) and `executing` (after interpretation) and `ModKind` (`None, Extended, StoppedShort, Declined, Stale`).
2. Regiment level: `issue_order(regiment, executing.order)` (SPEC-004 section 7). Otherwise the officer sends one `Order` to each direct subordinate, ascending id, same latitude, own `seq`: Move targets keep the formation's shape (`target + (sub centroid - formation centroid)`); Hold, Form and Face are copied.
**`interpret`** applies to Move only, and uses the officer's own picture, never the world. `Strict`: executed as received. `Discretionary`: `own` = formation strength, `threat` = sum over contacts within `practicable_radius_mm` of the target of `strength * confidence_pm / 1000`, `own_pm = 1000 * own / max(1, own + threat)`, `fat` = mean fatigue (ppm) of his men. `score = base_go_pm + sum(go_bias_pm) + (own_pm - 500) * odds_weight_pm / 1000 - fat * fatigue_weight_pm / 10^6 + judge_sigma_pm * gauss`. If `score < go_threshold_pm` the officer declines: `executing = Hold`, `Declined` (Ewell's "if practicable" on Cemetery Hill with tired troops, CWV p080, p307 trait). Otherwise the target moves along the line from the formation centroid by `sum(reach_mm)` (never behind the centroid; `Extended` or `StoppedShort`) and the gait shifts by `sum(gait_shift)`. One `gauss_unit` is always drawn.

## 7. Pictures, sightings and visibility
**Observation** every `observe_interval_ticks`: each regiment (ascending id) observes enemy regiments (ascending id), drawing from `STREAM_OBSERVE = 0x0C02`. Distance `d` between anchors; `range = sight_range_mm * post_range_pm / 1000`; skip if `d >= range`. `q = (1000 - 1000 * d / range) * (1000 - cover_pm(terrain at target)) / 1000 * (1000 - min(1000, smoke * smoke_occ_per_unit_pm)) / 1000`; skip if `q < min_quality_pm`. Draws (three `gauss_unit`, `g` the third): `sigma = pos_floor_mm + (1000 - q) * pos_sigma_max_mm / 1000` per axis; `strength_est = max(1, n * (1000 + (1000 - q) * strength_sigma_pm / 1000 * g / 1000) / 1000)`. The sighting enters the picture of the observer's Regiment officer at once.
**Contacts.** `Contact { id, pos, strength, quality_pm, seen, uncertainty_mm, intercepted }`. `confidence_pm(now) = quality_pm * max(0, stale_ticks - age) / stale_ticks`; `uncertainty_mm = 2 * sigma + age * drift_mm_per_tick`; dropped below `drop_pm`. A new sighting joins the nearest contact (lowest id on ties) within `assoc_base_mm + uncertainty_mm`, taking the newer position and a quality-weighted strength, else becomes a new contact. Contacts never carry the enemy `RegimentId`.
**Reports.** Each officer, at ticks where `(tick + id) % report_interval_ticks == 0`, sends a `Report` to his parent (by link if any, else courier): up to `report_max` contacts newer than `last_report` (confidence descending, id ascending) and one friend entry per regiment of his formation (`FriendEntry { regiment, pos, strength, seen }`, quality 1000 at the reporter, decaying as contacts do). The parent merges it with the same rule and forwards in his next report. Reports are not acknowledged.
**Views.** `picture_view(officer) -> PictureView { contacts, friends }` in ascending id (`ContactView { id, pos, radius_mm, strength, confidence_pm, age_ticks, intercepted }`). With `omniscient_view` it lists every living enemy regiment at confidence 1000, radius 0. The AI reads only `picture_view` and `order_views` of its own officer and orders only through `dispatch`: no information or speed the human lacks (checklist item 9).

## 8. Status feedback and streams
`order_views(root) -> Vec<OrderView { id, from, to, sent_tick, status, eta_lo, eta_hi, ack_deadline, sent, received, executing, truth }>`; `truth: Option<Truth>` (`InFlight, Delivered, Lost, Captured, Discarded`, plus the garble kind) is filled only with `omniscient_view` or after the battle. `ack_deadline = eta_hi + (return-trip eta_hi)`. Perceived status is a pure function of what has reached the sender: `Pending` until `sent + write`; with an ack, `Stale` if marked, else `Declined`, else `Misread` if `received != sent`, else `Modified` if `executing != received`, else `Delivered`; with no ack, `InTransit` until `ack_deadline`, `Overdue` until `+ lost_grace_ticks`, then `Lost` (presumed). A late ack replaces `Overdue` or `Lost`. Both bodies are always listed so the interface shows the difference. Only the addressee's ack reaches the issuer; lower hops show to their own senders and through the pictures.
**Streams.** Message `id` (below 2^24) uses `StreamId(0x5100_0000 + id)`, derived at creation and stored (hashed) in the message, so an unrelated message never changes another's outcome; `next_msg` increments per message. Observation uses `0x0C02`. Both are disjoint from SPEC-004 and SPEC-005 ids. Per message the draw order is: legs (3 words each), reading (2 words, 24 gauss words), staff jitter (1 word), `interpret` (12 words).

## 9. Inputs, wire format, tick order, hash
Replay input `actor`: `side` (human seat), `0x80 | side` (AI seat), `0xFE` (scenario script; REGISTRY section 2 owns the actor space, and only the seat actors are subject to the direct-order refusal of section 4). Payloads: `0x10` Dispatch = `u8 0x10, u8 medium (0 auto), u8 latitude, u16 from, ` then the unchanged SPEC-004 bytes `encode_order(RegimentId(to), order)` (the `u16` there names the addressee officer; tags 1 to 4 only); `0x11` is reserved; `0x12` Options = `u8 0x12, u8 side, u8 flags` (bit0 omniscient_view, bit1 instant_orders); `0x13` Script = `u8 0x13, u8 kind (1 officer down, 2 up, 3 link cut, 4 link restore), u16 a, u16 b`. Decoders reject truncation, trailing bytes and unknown values. `apply_command_input(actor, payload)` is called by the shell for the tick's inputs in `(actor, seq)` order before the step; a rejected input is logged and changes nothing.
**Tick order** (REGISTRY section 6): `step_command` is step TK-03, after the boundary and weather steps and before movement (positions, observation, reports, arrivals in ascending `(due, id)`, staff completions in ascending `(ready, id)`); the other steps follow in the registry order.
**Hash** (SPEC-004 amendment A2; block order and magics in REGISTRY section 5): when `cmd` is `Some`, after the SPEC-005 blocks and the morale block write `u32 0x434D_4431`, then every field of section 4 in declaration order (little-endian, fixed width, each `Vec` as `u32` length then ascending ids, orders as their wire bytes, `Option` as a `u8` tag). `None` writes nothing, so existing goldens hold.

## 13. Acceptance tests (tests first; tests are not edited to pass)
- **T7-01 loader:** `command.toml` and a scenario with officers and links load; the Ewell, Lee and Reynolds records give `T_LOW_DISC`, `T_HIGH_DISC`, 0; float, unknown key, missing provenance, cycle and double-owned regiment return the named errors; `validate_data.py` agrees.
- **T7-02 codec:** `decode_dispatch(encode_dispatch(..))` round-trips (proptest); bytes after the first five equal `encode_order(RegimentId(to), order)`; truncation, trailing bytes, unknown medium or latitude are rejected.
- **T7-03 latency:** `eta_lo` for ten fixed cases per medium equals `qc/fixtures/ref_command.py` (`Fraction`; the implementer runs it once and commits its output); with loss and garble zero and a stationary recipient, arrival lies in `[eta_lo, eta_hi]` for 1,000 seeds.
- **T7-04 media:** smoke-blocked flags wait, then fall back to courier after `smoke_max_waits`; a cut telegraph falls back at send; telegraph latency is equal at 1 km and 10 km.
- **T7-05 loss:** over seeds 1 to 2,000 the lost share is within 3 sigma of the formula, with and without 50 enemies on the path; `Captured` only with enemies, and it adds an intercepted contact to the enemy picture.
- **T7-06 garble:** garbled share within 3 sigma; kind shares match the thresholds; displacement deviation within 10 percent of `garble_sigma_mm`; the ack shows `Misread` iff `received != sent`.
- **T7-07 chain:** with loss, garble and `judge_sigma_pm` zero, an army Move to a corps of 3 x 3 x 3 regiments reaches all 27 with offsets preserved exactly and hop arrival ticks equal to the reference script.
- **T7-08 reinterpretation:** with `judge_sigma_pm = 0`, `Strict` executes as received for every mask; `Discretionary` aggressive goes with `+200000 mm` and gait +1, cautious with `-200000 mm`; a low-discretion formation at fatigue 800000 and `own_pm` 450 declines (`Hold`, `Declined`); scores equal a hand-derived table in the test.
- **T7-09 stale:** order A by slow courier, countermand B by telegraph arriving first: A is discarded, the regiment follows B, A's status is `Stale`.
- **T7-10 picture:** in-range regiments appear, others do not; woods and smoke lower `q`; confidence follows the formula and contacts drop below `drop_pm`; a contact needs one report hop per level to reach the army.
- **T7-11 visibility:** AI and human dispatches with equal inputs deliver on equal ticks; with no sighting the picture has no enemy contact; `omniscient_view` lists all enemies at 1000; `instant_orders` delivers next tick and leaves message streams unchanged; direct regiment inputs are refused when `cmd` is `Some`.

- **T7-14 succession:** a message to a down officer reaches his deputy after `redirect_ticks`; with no deputy it is lost.
- **T7-15 bands:** `run_command_bands` (seeds 1 to 64) puts every CT-007-1, CT-007-2, CT-007-3, CT-007-6 and CT-007-7 quantity in its band; min and max are printed.

## Amendment A1 (from T-074 implementation, 2026-11-04)
1. Section 4, state: `CommandState` gains `staff: Vec<StaffJob>`, the orders that have been read and wait for the staff of their addressee, ascending by `(ready, id)`. `StaffJob { ready: u64, id: MessageId, from: OfficerId, to: OfficerId, seq: u32, received: OrderBody, stale: bool, instant: bool, rng: [u64; 4] }`: `id` is the order's message and the key of its `OrderRecord`, `from` the issuer, `to` the officer whose staff works on it, `received` the body after garbling, `rng` the message stream after the reading draws and the staff draw. `OrderRecord` gains `ack_deadline: u64` and `medium: Medium`.
2. Section 9, hash: after the `pics` block the command block writes `staff` as a `u32` length, then per job `ready` (`u64`), `id` (`u32`), `from` (`u16`), `to` (`u16`), `seq` (`u32`), `received` (the order's wire bytes then the latitude `u8`), `stale` (`u8`, 0 or 1), `instant` (`u8`, 0 or 1) and the four `rng` words (`u64` each), all little-endian. In each record, `ack_deadline` (`u64`) and the medium tag (`u8`) follow `eta_hi` and precede `sent`. `None` still writes nothing.
3. Section 8, `ack_deadline`: fixed when the order is sent as `eta_hi` plus the latest estimate, taken at that moment, of an ack of `ACK_WORDS = 6` words from the addressee back to the sender by the automatic medium (`eta_hi - tick`). `Pending` lasts until `sent_tick + write_ticks` of the record's medium; `InTransit` through `ack_deadline`; `Overdue` through `ack_deadline + lost_grace_ticks`; then `Lost`.
4. Section 8, views: `order_views(table, issuer)` lists the records whose `from` is `issuer`, ascending by id (the `root` of the section 8 text is the issuing officer). `OrderView` carries `sent`, `received` and `executing` (the last two once the ack has arrived), and `truth` with the garble kind as a second field, filled only with the side's `omniscient_view`; `order_views_final` always fills them, for the end of the battle. The ack of an order is matched to its record by the ack's `root`; the first ack stands.
5. Section 6, chain: every order that is read, stale ones included, waits its staff time; the jitter word is drawn right after the reading draws. `process_pm` of a mask is 1000 multiplied by each set trait's `process_pm / 1000` in turn, and the staff time is `staff_ticks * process_pm / 1000`. A stale order is acknowledged with `Stale` and passed on to no one. `interpret` draws its one `gauss_unit` for every order that is not stale and not instant, whatever its kind or latitude. The go score truncates each division toward zero and shifts the judgement product right by 32. The threat counts the contacts of the officer's own picture, the omniscient view not included, whose confidence is at least `drop_pm`. The gait shift stays within walk to double-quick, and a faster gait is kept.
