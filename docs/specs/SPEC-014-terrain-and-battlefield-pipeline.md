# SPEC-014: terrain and battlefield pipeline (survey data to a playable battlefield)

> Shared namespaces (input tags and actors, random streams, flags, hash blocks, tick order, CT ids, amendment labels, ticket ids) are owned by [REGISTRY.md](REGISTRY.md); conflicts and their resolutions are in [CONFLICTS.md](CONFLICTS.md).

New crate `crates/terrain` (depends on `core-math` only; `proptest` dev; `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]`; no I/O, no clocks), new shell `shells/terrain-cli` (binary `hd-terrain`, the data compiler), new data under `data/survey/` (survey truth), `data/terrain/` (compiled packs) and `qc/terrain/`. Source key: **CWV pNNN** = the research volume, PDF page NNN, with the page's own tag. An invented number is `placeholder` with a calibration target (CT-014-n, section 13). Units are mm, cm, dm, permille (`_pm`) and ppm as in SPEC-004 section 1; `Fx` appears only at the API.

## 1. Purpose and scope
Turn survey elevation, period maps and records into a deterministic battlefield the simulation reads: height grid, cover and movement classes, a linear obstacle layer and sight data, for about 100 km2 in tiles, starting with a slab of about 4 km2 (Round Tops, Wheatfield). Real terrain is the base but is not automatically good gameplay terrain, so changes are recorded data with reasons (CWV p464, untagged: "hand-edit for gameplay"). Out of scope: rendering, vegetation, weather, deformation, path-finding.

## 2. Definitions
- **Survey truth**: elevation as measured and July 1863 land cover as mapped, with provenance; never hand-edited.
- **Play pack**: survey truth plus recorded adjustments; the only terrain the simulation reads.
- **Tile**: 128 x 128 cells of `CELL_MM = 4000`; `TILE_MM = 512000` (0.262 km2). 100 km2 is about 400 tiles; 16 tiles are 4.19 km2. **Sector**: a named tile set for polish and review.
- **World frame**: x east, y north, mm; origin is the south-west corner of tile (0,0); z is elevation above `z_origin_cm`. Cell `(cx,cy)` covers `[cx*4000, (cx+1)*4000)`; heights sit at cell corners (vertices).

## 3. Survey layer and its verification
**Elevation.** Public-domain lidar-derived bare-earth elevation where coverage allows (plan section 4.2); assumed a US federal 1 m or better product, **not yet verified** (section 14). `data/survey/gettysburg/sources.toml` has one `[[source]]` per input: `id` (u16, nonzero), `kind` (dem, map, photo, record), `provider`, `product`, `acquired`, `retrieved` (ISO dates), `data_sha256`, `licence_status` (`pending|cleared|refused`), `licence_basis`, `licence_evidence_sha256` (hash of the saved licence or policy text), `cleared_by` (human id, gate G1), `vertical_rmse_mm`, and for maps `georef_rms_mm` with `[[control]]` points. Procedure: (1) record the provider's index query and its result hash; (2) `ingest_dem.py` (pinned GDAL) writes `dem_cm.i32` (1 m posts, local frame, centimetres, void `i32::MIN`) and `coverage.u8` (source id per post, 0 none), both hashed in the manifest; (3) a human sets `licence_status = "cleared"` with evidence; (4) `hd-terrain check` fails on any uncleared source, any slab post with source 0, or voids above 1000 ppm. Ingest is the only floating-point step; everything after is integer and a pure function of hashed inputs.
**Modern change.** Bare earth may still show modern regrading, fills and ponds (assumption); each is reverted by a `restore` adjustment with period-map evidence.
**Land cover and linear features, July 1863.** Digitised from period maps, surveys and records into integer-mm TOML (`landcover.toml`, `linear.toml`, `buildings.toml`, `names.toml`); every row has `confidence`, `sources` (a source id plus page or sheet) and, above `unverified`, a `second_source`. Polygon kinds are the class names of section 4.3; linear kinds: `road` and `creek` (`half_width_mm`; creek flow = vertex order), `stone_wall`, `rail_fence`, `board_fence`, `hedge`, `earthwork`. `names.toml` rows (id, kind, anchor point, optional polygon) cover the features the research lists: McPherson, Seminary and Oak Ridges, Cemetery Hill and Ridge, Culp's Hill, Little and Big Round Top, Wheatfield, Peach Orchard, Devil's Den, Plum Run, Emmitsburg Road ridge, Spangler's Spring, Rock Creek (CWV p079, untagged), Rose Woods, Stony Hill, Triangular Field, Slaughter Pen (CWV p082, untagged). Town streets are `road` rows; town buildings are footprints with `height_mm`. Facts carried: the Wheatfield is 20 acres between Rose Woods and the Stony Hill (CWV p082, untagged); the town is a hub of about ten roads (CWV p079, p385, untagged).
**Geometry rules** (enforced by `hd-terrain check`; error names in brackets). A ring lists vertices without repeating the first: at least 3 [`ring_short`], no zero-length edge [`edge_zero`], non-zero area [`ring_flat`], no crossing or touching of non-adjacent edges by exact `i128` cross products [`ring_self_touch`], outer counter-clockwise and holes clockwise [`ring_winding`], each hole strictly inside its outer ring [`hole_outside`]. Polygons of one group (`ground`, `water`, `structure`) may not overlap [`group_overlap`]. A polyline has at least 2 vertices, no zero-length segment [`line_short`]; a road end lies within 1000 mm of another road or the extent edge, or has `dead_end = true` [`road_dangling`]; a creek never rises along flow by more than `creek_rise_tol_cm` (CT-014-6) between consecutive vertices of the play pack [`creek_uphill`], which also tests map-to-elevation registration.

## 4. Compiled format
### 4.1 Tile file `.hdt` (little-endian, versioned)
`magic "HDTL"`, `version u16 = 1`, `tile_x u16`, `tile_y u16`, `cells u16 = 128`, `cell_mm u32 = 4000`, `sections u8 = 5`, sections in id order (`id u8, len u32, payload`), `body_hash u64` (FNV-1a 64 of all preceding bytes, as SPEC-002).
| id | payload |
|---|---|
| 1 height | 129 x 129 `u16`, row-major, row 0 south, cm above `z_origin_cm`; edges duplicated in neighbours |
| 2 class | 128 x 128 `u8`, a SPEC-004 terrain class id |
| 3 occ | 128 x 128 `u8`, height of sight-blocking cover above ground, dm |
| 4 flags | 128 x 128 `u8`: bit0 `NO_SURVEY`, bit1 `ADJ_RESTORE`, bit2 `ADJ_PLAY`, bit3 `POLISH`, bit4 `STEEP` |
| 5 obstacles | `u32 n`, then `n` records ascending by `seg_id`: `seg_id u32, kind u8, flags u8, height_mm u16, ax i32, ay i32, bx i32, by i32` (mm, world frame); a segment is stored in every tile its bounding box touches |
The decoder rejects bad magic, unknown version, wrong `cells`/`cell_mm`, truncation, trailing bytes, missing or duplicate sections, wrong section length, unsorted or duplicate obstacles, and hash mismatch; it never panics and checks any declared length against the remaining bytes before allocating.
### 4.2 Index `index.toml` (integer-only TOML, ADR 0004)
`schema = "terrain-index/1"`, `cell_mm`, `tile_cells`, `tiles_x`, `tiles_y`, `origin_easting_mm`, `origin_northing_mm`, `z_origin_cm` (lowest vertex; compile fails if relief exceeds 65,535 cm), `crs` (text; assumed UTM zone 18N, NAD83(2011), NAVD88), `legend` (class id to name), `[[tile]] x, y, hash_hex` (hex string: TOML integers are `i64`), `[[sector]] id, tiles`, `[[slab]] id, x0, y0, x1, y1`. `content_hash()` is FNV-1a 64 over `cell_mm`, `tiles_x`, `tiles_y`, `z_origin_cm` and tile hashes in ascending `(y, x)`. A scenario names its pack with `[terrain_pack] name, index_hash_hex`; the CLI refuses a mismatch.
### 4.3 Classes and obstacle kinds (placeholders)
SPEC-004 classes 0 to 7 are unchanged. SPEC-004 amendment A3 (REGISTRY section 9) appends (CT-014-3): `8 crop` (speed 900, effort 1100, disorder 120, cover 50), `9 rocky` (500, 1600, 450, 400), `10 yard` (800, 1100, 200, 100), `11 building` (speed 0, effort 1000, disorder 0, cover 0, blocks). Raster priority, lowest first: open, crop, orchard, woods, thicket, rocky, marsh, yard, deep_water, ford, road, building. Default `occ_dm` (CT-014-2): open, road, ford, deep_water 0; marsh 10; crop 10; rocky 12; yard 20; thicket 25; orchard 45; building 70; woods 180; a polygon may override.
`data/rules/obstacles.toml` (SPEC-004 section 2 provenance): `[[kind]] id, name, height_mm, fire_blocking, sight_blocking, crossing_ticks`, all CT-014-4: 1 stone_wall (1000, true, false, 60), 2 rail_fence (1300, false, false, 40), 3 board_fence (1500, false, false, 50), 4 hedge (1500, false, true, 80), 5 earthwork (1100, true, false, 100), 6 building_wall (per building, true, true, 0). Walls give cover at Gettysburg (CWV p511, untagged).

## 5. Algorithms (integer only)
**Compile** (`hd-terrain compile`; inputs sorted by `(layer, id)`):
1. Heights: vertex `(vx,vy)` at `(vx*4000, vy*4000)` takes the mean of the 16 one-metre posts within 2000 mm, `(sum + 8) / 16` (`i64`); void posts are skipped, and the vertex is `NO_SURVEY` if fewer than 8 are valid.
2. Adjustments (section 6), ascending `id`.
3. Classes: cells start `open`; polygons apply in priority order then ascending id. A polygon takes a cell when the centre is inside by the even-odd rule with half-open edges (`ay <= py < by`, side by an `i128` cross product), minus holes. A road or creek polyline takes a cell when the exact centre-to-line distance is at most `half_width_mm` (`cross^2 <= hw^2 * len^2`; end caps by squared distance). A creek channel is `ford` with a `marsh` margin; ponds are `deep_water`. Buildings also emit one `building_wall` segment per footprint edge.
4. Obstacles: polylines become segments, `seg_id = feature_id * 1024 + k`; kind heights apply unless the row overrides.
5. Pack: tiles, index, hashes; unadjusted `survey` tiles are written beside the pack for QC only.
**Queries** (`TerrainWorld`). `mm = (raw as i128 * 1000) >> 32` (floor). Positions outside the extent clamp to the nearest edge cell (as SPEC-004 section 5). `height_mm(p)`: `C = 4000`, `fx, fy` offsets in the cell, `h = floor(10 * (h00*(C-fx)*(C-fy) + h10*fx*(C-fy) + h01*(C-fx)*fy + h11*fx*fy) / C^2)` in `i128`. `slope_pm(cell)`: `gx = ((h10+h11)-(h00+h01))/2` (cm, truncating), `gy` likewise, `isqrt(gx^2+gy^2) * 10 * 1000 / C` (floor `isqrt`).
**Line of sight** `los(from, from_z_mm, to, to_z_mm, mode)`, `z` above local ground. `D = isqrt(dx^2+dy^2)`, `n = max(1, ceil(D / 2000))` (`SIGHT_STEP_MM`, CT-014-5), `A0 = height(from)+from_z`, `A1 = height(to)+to_z`. For `k = 1..n-1`: `p_k = from + floor((to-from)*k/n)` per axis, `ray_k = A0 + floor((A1-A0)*k/n)`. Ground blocks if `height(p_k) > ray_k`; mode `Sight` also if `height(p_k) + 100*occ(cell of p_k) > ray_k`, ignoring the cells of `from` and `to`. Obstacles (`fire_blocking` kinds for `Fire`, `sight_blocking` for `Sight`): exact 2D segment intersection (`i128`) gives rational `t`; blocked if `floor(A0 + (A1-A0)*t) <= height(at the intersection, floor mm) + height_mm`, matching SPEC-005 section 5 (`z(s) <= height`). The result is the nearest blocker along the ray (ground samples at `k*D/n`; obstacle first on a tie). `LosResult { clear: bool, blocked_at_mm: i64, blocker: Blocker }`.

## 6. Adjustments and gameplay-versus-reality records
`adjustments.toml`: `[[adjustment]] id, category (restore|play|repair), op, polygon, feather_mm` plus op integers: `raise`/`lower` (`delta_cm`), `flatten` (`target_cm`), `set_class` (`class`), `set_occ` (`occ_dm`), `add_obstacle`/`remove_obstacle` (`feature_id`). Every row has a non-empty `reason` and provenance. A `play` row also has `reality` and `game` integers and `value_unit`. Feathering is a linear blend by integer distance to the ring. Budget (CT-014-6): `play` rows move no vertex more than `play_max_delta_cm = 150`, touch at most `play_area_share_ppm = 20000` of vertices, and never move a named summit. `restore` rows are exempt from the budget but need a `reason` citing the period source. `hd-terrain report` writes `qc/terrain/adjustments.csv` (id, category, vertices, max delta, volume) and sets the `ADJ_*` bits.

## 7. Polish zones kept apart from truth
Artist work lives under `art/terrain-polish/<sector>/` (render heightmaps, splat maps, instance lists) with a `zone.toml` (`sector`, `polygon`, `polish_max_delta_mm`, CT-014-7, placeholder 500). The simulation, compiler and hashes never read it. `hd-terrain polish-check` fails if a polished heightmap differs from the play pack by more than the limit inside the zone or at all outside it, if `zone.toml` has a key beginning `sim_`, or if a change would alter class or obstacles; such a change must return as an adjustment row. The `POLISH` flag is for review only.

## 8. Sector tiling and streaming
Tiles are the unit of decode and residency. `required_tiles` is a pure function of agent positions plus `stream_margin_mm` (CT-014-8, placeholder 1000000, at least the longest equipped weapon reach). Residency changes speed only: a non-resident tile returns `TerrainError::TileMissing(TileId)`; the shell makes every required tile resident before each tick; the simulation never loads. Insertion and eviction order never change an answer. The pack `content_hash`, not the resident set, identifies terrain in replays and goldens. Hypothesis: all 400 tiles fit resident (about 36 MB).

## 9. Initial slab
`[[slab]] id = "round_tops_wheatfield"`: whole tiles, area 3.5 to 5.3 km2 (CT-014-1 band, "about 4 km2"), containing the anchors of Little and Big Round Top, Devil's Den, Plum Run, Wheatfield, Rose Woods, Stony Hill, Triangular Field, Slaughter Pen and Peach Orchard with at least 150 m margin. Exact tiles and origin are recorded by the first ticket that has georeferenced anchors. The slab is the first deliverable and is committed (`data/terrain/gettysburg-slab/`, about 1.5 MB); the full pack is a build product.

## 10. Tool outline
`tools/terrain/ingest_dem.py`; `tools/validate_data.py` extended to survey schemas and `sources.toml`; `hd-terrain check | compile | export-rows | los | report | polish-check | verify`. `export-rows --tiles x0,y0,x1,y1 --cell-mm N` (the 8000 mm form is what the simulation grids read: `TerrainGrid` of SPEC-004, `SmokeGrid` and `ElevationGrid` of SPEC-009, whose cell must equal the terrain cell; the 4000 mm pack is read through `TerrainWorld`; REGISTRY section 13) writes a SPEC-004 `[terrain]` table (single-character `legend`, `rows` with row 0 south); for `N = 8000` a cell takes the most frequent class of its four sub-cells, ties to higher priority. `verify` recompiles and compares tile hashes. Files are processed in ascending id.

## 11. Public API (`crates/terrain`, exact)
`CELL_MM: i64`, `TILE_CELLS: u32`, `TILE_MM: i64`, `SIGHT_STEP_MM: i64`; `TileId { x: u16, y: u16 }` (orders by `(y, x)`); `TerrainError` (`Format(String)`, `Hash`, `TileMissing(TileId)`, `OutOfExtent`, `Index(String)`); `Tile::{decode(&[u8]) -> Result<Tile, TerrainError>, encode(&self) -> Vec<u8>, id}`; `ObstacleSeg { id: u32, kind: u8, flags: u8, height_mm: u16, a: [i32; 2], b: [i32; 2] }` with `endpoints(&self) -> (Vec2Fx, Vec2Fx)`; `TerrainIndex::{from_toml_str(&str), content_hash(&self) -> u64, tile_hash(TileId) -> Option<u64>}`; `TerrainWorld::{new(TerrainIndex), insert_tile(&mut self, &[u8]) -> Result<TileId, TerrainError>, evict_tile(&mut self, TileId) -> bool, is_resident, resident_ids() -> Vec<TileId>, tiles_for_rect(min: Vec2Fx, max: Vec2Fx) -> Vec<TileId>, height_mm(Vec2Fx) -> Result<i64, _>, class_at(Vec2Fx) -> Result<u8, _>, occ_mm, flags_at, slope_pm, obstacles_in(min, max, &mut Vec<ObstacleSeg>) -> Result<(), _>, los(from: Vec2Fx, from_z_mm: i64, to: Vec2Fx, to_z_mm: i64, mode: LosMode) -> Result<LosResult, _>}`; `LosMode::{Fire, Sight}`; `Blocker::{None, Ground, Occluder, Obstacle(u32)}`. `obstacles_in` returns ascending `seg_id`, no duplicates. Glue to SPEC-005 maps `ObstacleSeg` to `Obstacle { a, b, height_mm }` and keeps only `fire_blocking` kinds.

## 12. Acceptance tests (tests first; thresholds are not edited to pass)
- **T14-01 codec:** round trip (proptest); each section 4.1 rejection returns `Format` or `Hash`; fuzzed bytes never panic; two encodes are byte-equal.
- **T14-02 height:** `height_mm` at each vertex is `10 * h_cm`; at a cell centre equals `10 * (h00+h10+h01+h11) / 4` floored; equal across tile seams; 10,000 seeded points equal `qc/fixtures/ref_height.py` (`Fraction`, floor), recorded once.
- **T14-03 classes and compatibility:** `class_at` clamps outside the extent; `export-rows` at 4000 mm loaded by SPEC-004 `TerrainGrid::from_rows` gives the same `class_at` as `TerrainWorld::class_at` at every slab cell centre; classes 8 to 11 load from `terrain.toml`, and `blocks_movement` is true only for 7 and 11.
- **T14-04 analytic sight:** flat ground is clear; a ridge of height `H` midway blocks eye `H - 1` and passes `H + 1`; a 1000 mm wall at fraction `f` of a 1700 mm to 450 mm line blocks iff `1700 - 1250 f <= 1000`; `Sight` blocks through woods that `Fire` passes; own-cell occluders are ignored.
- **T14-05 sight reference:** `los` equals `qc/fixtures/ref_los.py` (independent, Python integers, section 5 rule) on the vantage pairs and 1,000 seeded slab pairs, both modes, output committed.
- **T14-06 geometry:** each negative fixture (bow-tie, repeated closing vertex, zero-area ring, touching hole, clockwise outer, overlapping same-group polygons, one-point line, dangling road, uphill creek) yields exactly its named error; slab data yield none.
- **T14-07 raster:** hand-computed cell sets for a square, a triangle, a ring with a hole and a 6 m road match; shuffled input rows give a byte-equal pack.
- **T14-08 determinism:** slab compile twice, in two processes and on three CI platforms gives one `content_hash`, recorded once in `qc/golden-replays/expected.json` (`terrain` section) and checked by `tools/verify_goldens.sh`.
- **T14-09 adjustments:** play-vs-survey differences obey the CT-014-6 budget; every row has `reason`; `play` rows have `reality`, `game`, `value_unit`; summits unchanged; `adjustments.csv` reproduces.
- **T14-10 slope:** a 1000 mm rise over 4000 mm gives `slope_pm = 250`; on the slab every connected group of at least 4 cells with `slope_pm >= steep_pm` (CT-014-9) lies inside a `[[steep_ok]]` polygon with a reason.
- **T14-11 named features:** Wheatfield area is 80,937 m2 plus or minus 25 percent (CT-014-10; 20 acres at 4046.8564224 m2, CWV p082); `d(WF,RW) + d(WF,SH) <= 1.5 * d(RW,SH)` (CWV p082). Both gate only if the polygon has a `second_source`, else reported. On the full pack, `road` centrelines crossing a circle of `town_circle_mm` (CT-014-10, 1500 m) about the town anchor number 8 to 12 (about ten, CWV p079, p385), reported only.
- **T14-12 vantage:** `qc/terrain/vantage.toml` rows `{a, b, a_eye_mm, b_eye_mm, mode, expect, basis, confidence}`; `verified` rows with a `second_source` gate, others report (plan section 11, terrain truth). Rows are recorded from named accounts, including Warren's view from Little Round Top (CWV p081, untagged).
- **T14-13 streaming:** 100,000 seeded queries (height, class, `los`, `obstacles_in`) are identical with all tiles resident and with at most 4 (seeded insert/evict); a missing tile returns `TileMissing` with the lowest missing id; `tiles_for_rect` is ascending and clamped.
- **T14-14 polish:** a within-limit fixture passes; one vertex over, one outside the zone, a `sim_` key and a class change each fail; the pack hash is unchanged with and without `art/`.
- **T14-15 sources:** a `pending` or `refused` source, a slab post with source 0, or voids above 1000 ppm fail `check`; every shipped source is `cleared` with evidence hash and `cleared_by`.
- **T14-16 perf (ignored, prints numbers):** section 14 budgets.

## 13. Calibration targets
CT-014-1 resolution: LOS on the compiled grid agrees with LOS on the 1 m posts for at least 98 percent of 10,000 slab pairs, else the cell size is revisited; slab area band. CT-014-2 occluder heights against period accounts with a second source. CT-014-3 classes 8 to 11, with CT-004-4 (woods at most 50 percent of open speed). CT-014-4 obstacle heights, crossing times. CT-014-5 sight step. CT-014-6 play budget and `creek_rise_tol_cm` (placeholder 100). CT-014-7 polish drift. CT-014-8 stream margin. CT-014-9 `steep_pm` (placeholder 800). CT-014-10 named-feature tolerances. CT-014-11 map registration limit `georef_rms_mm` (placeholder 20000).

## 14. Performance (hypothesis), assumptions, risks
H14: `height_mm` at most 80 ns; `los` over 2 km at most 100 us; tile decode at most 1 ms; slab compile at most 120 s, full pack 30 min; resident full pack at most 48 MB.
**Assumptions.** Public-domain bare-earth lidar coverage of all 100 km2 at 1 m or better exists (unverified; ticket T-141 decides at gate G1). The frame is UTM 18N, NAD83(2011), NAVD88 (unverified). Period maps register to about 20 m (unverified). Relief is below 655 m.
**Risks.** (1) Licence or coverage fails and a coarser source forces a new CT-014-1. (2) 4 m cells smooth boulder fields; rock cover is a class number. (3) Occluder-by-cell sight is coarse in woods. (4) Map registration error misplaces walls; checks catch only some.
**Required amendments (spec first, in T-141).** SPEC-004 amendment A3 (section 5): classes 8 to 11; slope is data only, not yet used by movement. SPEC-005 amendment A16, reserved and blocked on open decision D-02 in CONFLICTS (the labels are in REGISTRY section 9): sections 2 and 5 assume ground at z = 0; with this pack, ground contact is `z <= height_mm(x,y)`, obstacles span `ground + height_mm`, the muzzle is `ground + posture height`, hit heights are ground-relative. Non-`fire_blocking` kinds are not passed to SPEC-005.
